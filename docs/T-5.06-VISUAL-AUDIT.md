# T-5.06 Homepage Audit

Measured 2026-11-20 with Chromium headless (CDP) against a production build
(`next build` + `next start -p 3110`). Every number below is a real browser
measurement or a painted screenshot pixel, not an estimate from source.

## 1. Two requirements in the task brief that do not exist

| Brief says | Found |
|---|---|
| Homepage "menampilkan proyek terbaru pada perangkat pengguna" | **Tidak ditemukan.** The only `localStorage` keys in the repository are `theme_preference` (`app/layout.tsx`, `theme-toggle.tsx`) and `rail_collapsed` (`workspace-shell.tsx`). No created project is ever stored. `Home` holds `title`, `deadline`, `loading`, `error`, `successData` and nothing else. `handleSubmit` POSTs once to `/api/projects` and renders the returned code; there is no list, no history, no persistence. |
| "PRD Bagian 12.2, 12.3, 12.6, 12.7" | **Tidak ditemukan.** `prd.md` v0.2 has 9 top-level sections; there is no §12. Design guidance is `DESIGN.md`. Motion limits live in PRD §6.9 (NFR-46 to NFR-50): transform and opacity only, honour `prefers-reduced-motion`, no animation may block interaction, no decorative animation without function. |

The brief also says the homepage should be "selaraskan glass surface dengan token T-5.05". The
homepage renders **no** glass plate: `grep` for `backdrop-blur|rail-surface` in `app/page.tsx` returns
zero matches. The `--rail-*` tokens belong to the workspace rail, which is not on this route. The only
translucent surface is the sticky navbar (`bg-paper/85 backdrop-blur-md backdrop-saturate-150`); the
form panel is opaque `bg-card` with a stacked shadow.

Decided with the user: report the absent feature rather than invent it (PRD §7.5 rule 6: do not build
features that have no functional requirement; the brief also forbids dummy data), and scope the glass
work to the two surfaces the homepage actually has, the navbar and the form panel.

## 3. Measured defects

### 3.1 The form is below the fold on every mobile viewport

`formFullyInFold` is the hard test: is the whole form visible without scrolling?

| Viewport | Viewport height | Form top | Form bottom | Form fully in fold | Main content before the form |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320 | 812 | **1308** | 1522 | **false** | eyebrow, h1, description, formula plate, 3 limits, CTA row |
| 375 | 812 | **1191** | 1405 | **false** | same |
| 390 | 812 | **1140** | 1354 | **false** | same |
| 768 | 900 | **943** | 1158 | **false** | same |
| 1024 | 900 | 298 | 513 | true | (two-column layout starts here) |
| 1280 | 900 | 298 | 513 | true | same |

At 390px the form starts **1140px down**, 350px past the bottom of the viewport. To reach the page's
only action the user scrolls past an eyebrow, a 170px headline, a 153px paragraph, an 86px formula
plate, a 201px stats block, and a 68px CTA row: **~1000px of reading before the first input.**
The brief states "Jangan membuat hero dekoratif yang menggeser aksi utama terlalu jauh ke bawah."
This is the single largest defect on the page and it is a layout problem, not a styling one.

### 3.2 The mobile "form" is actually second

The `<h1>` occupies the top of the page and the form is in the second grid cell. On mobile the grid
is one column, so document order puts every explanatory block between the headline and the form.
Nothing is wrong with the *content* order (headline then action is right), but the *stack* places four
blocks in between that never earn that position.

### 3.3 The limits block stacks into three full-width rows and costs 201px

`.grid-cols-1 ... sm:grid-cols-3` means 320 to 639px renders "Formula / Batas beban / Anggota" as
three stacked rows with a hairline rule above and below. It is 201px tall on mobile for three
3-word facts. On desktop the same block is 84px, so the mobile height is not intrinsic, it is the
column count.

### 3.4 The panel's azure wash reads as a generic soft halo in light mode

`bg-accent-tint/70` on an absolutely positioned `-inset-x-3 -top-3 bottom-3` box with `rounded-[32px]`.
In light mode (`--accent-tint: #eaf0ff`) that is a large pale lavender sheet behind an opaque white
card: it lowers separation between panel and canvas instead of raising it, and it is the one element
on the page that looks like a generic "glow behind the card" template. In dark mode it is much better
because the tint is dark.

### 3.5 Navbar and panel glass do not share a material

The navbar uses `bg-paper/85` + `backdrop-blur-md` + `backdrop-saturate-150` and a `border-line/70`
hairline; the panel uses an opaque `bg-card` plus a two-layer shadow. They are the two surfaces that
sit above the canvas and they read as different materials. There is no rim light on either, no
shared edge token, and no hover response on the panel.

### 3.6 Desktop: the left column and the panel do not share a baseline

Measured at 1440x900: the left column starts at `138` (eyebrow `145`), the panel starts at `149`
(its outer wash `-top-3`). Neither aligns to the other nor to a common grid line, so the fold looks
slightly slipped rather than composed. The left column's internal gaps are `13px` before the
description and `39px` after it, which is an accident of `space-y-7` interacting with a nested
`space-y-7`, not a decision.

### 3.7 Wasted vertical space inside the bands

The `.band-recessed` and `.band-open` sections carry `py-16` / `lg:py-24`, and the hero carries
`pt-20 lg:pb-24`. On desktop the page is 2406px tall for two short sections. Between the end of the
`AlurProduk` step copy and the section's bottom edge there is roughly 100px of empty band before the
next section's top edge, twice. It reads as padding rather than as composition.

### 3.8 What is already good and must not regress

- `handleSubmit` validation (empty title, >100 chars, empty deadline), the `aria-invalid` /
  `aria-describedby` wiring, the live region, the error text next to its own field.
- The success state: code, title, deadline, and the "Kelola anggota" link to `successData.link`.
- Navbar links (`/how-it-works`, `#scoring`), `ThemeToggle`, `SectionRail` anchors.
- `FloatShell` stopping permanently on first pointer/focus (a drifting form is hostile).
- Every `MaskLine` / `RevealGroup` / scroll-linked value, and the `reduced` guards on all of them.
- The `prefers-reduced-motion` first-paint rule in `globals.css`.

## 4. Plan, within the 5-file limit

| File | Why |
|---|---|
| `app/page.tsx` | composition: hero order on mobile, limits row, panel material, navbar glass |
| `app/globals.css` | shared glass tokens for navbar + panel, spacing scale, band padding |

Nothing else. `primitives.tsx` is shared with other pages, `reveal.tsx` already does what is needed,
and the workspace pages, `lib/`, `app/api/`, and `supabase/` are out of scope.

### Direction

1. **Pull the action up on mobile without deleting any content.** The form panel moves above the
   formula plate and the limits block, so the first screen is headline, description, and the form.
   The explanatory blocks keep their order and text; they simply stop standing between the reader and
   the one thing the page is for. On desktop nothing moves: the panel is already the right column.
2. **Collapse the limits block to a 3-column row from the smallest width**, since each cell is three
   words. That alone returns about 120px on mobile.
3. **Replace the panel's lavender halo with a real glass material** built from the existing palette:
   translucent card face, backdrop blur, a 1px edge that lightens on hover, and a single contact
   shadow. No new hue.
4. **Give the navbar and the panel the same edge token** so the two floating surfaces read as one
   family.
5. **Tighten the vertical rhythm** of the two lower bands and align the desktop panel's top with the
   left column's first line.
6. Motion: keep every existing gesture. Shorten the hero's total choreography so the panel is
   settled sooner (it is the action, it should not arrive last at 460ms plus a 620ms line), and make
   the hero stagger depend on the block, not on a fixed list of delays.
